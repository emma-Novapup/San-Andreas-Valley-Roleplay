Our Mission

The mission of the San Andreas State Police is to safeguard lives and property throughout the state. We enforce traffic regulations, combat highway crime, investigate major incidents, and supply strategic tactical support to local municipalities. As the premier law enforcement branch in the Valley, we seek professionals committed to bravery, duty, and community trust.

Specialized Divisions

State Highway Patrol

Enforcing safety on state routes and federal freeways. Equipped with high-speed pursuit interceptors and radar packages to ensure traffic flow and general transit security.

Tactical Response (SRT)

Specialized Enforcement Unit handling high-risk scenarios. Highly trained officers focusing on barricaded suspects, crisis negotiation, and advanced weapons handling.

K-9 & Air Support

Advanced tactical assets featuring drug-detection/tracking K-9 units and Air Support choppers equipped with high-beam spotlights and thermal camera optics.
